Anno Domini MLXVI

Battle for Britain

The Norman Invasion

A scrolling campaign in the manner of Chris Crawford’s Eastern Front

How to play

You are William, Duke of Normandy. Issue orders to your host, then execute the week. English and Norse orders resolve at the same time as yours.

  • Move the pink cursor with the arrow keys or WASD. The map scrolls at the edges of the screen.
  • Enter picks up a Norman unit. Further arrow keys plot up to eight marching orders. Enter puts it down.
  • Click a unit, then click a destination, to plot a path. Backspace undoes one order; Space clears them.
  • T (or Execute week) runs thirty-two ticks of movement and combat. Space skips the animation.
  • March a land unit onto a friendly fleet at the coast to embark. Order it onto an English beach to land.
  • Gales close the Channel. Harold will march north on Hardrada first. That is your opening.
  • Hold London, and Winchester or Harold’s death, before Christmas.

The campaign is ended
